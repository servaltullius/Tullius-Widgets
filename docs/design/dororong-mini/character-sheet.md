# Dororong (Doro) — character sheet for the mini HUD icons

**Target style: "도로일기콘" (Doro diary emoticons)** — the user's favourite Doro format. Reference stills (not shipped): `view/.qa/dororong-mini/ref/`.
Background research: `docs/plans/2026-10-12-dororong-research.ko.md` (Doro is a fan-made meme character derived from Dorothy of *Goddess of Victory: NIKKE*, now owned by SHIFT UP; no official model sheet).

## Must-have traits
- **Hair**: soft pink (#F4A7C8) bob with a few spiky strands on top and side locks framing the face.
- **Rose buns on BOTH sides**: a pink spiral-rose bun on each side of the head (same pink as the hair), each tied with a **light sky-blue ribbon** (#9FD3F0). Not purple.
- **Eyes**: big round lavender-violet eyes (#9F8FE0) with large white highlights; **thick dark straight/slightly furrowed brows** that give a smug, determined look.
- **Mouth**: cat mouth "ω", often open with a **small pink tongue** sticking out; soft round pink blush on the cheeks.
- **Body**: a **white, upright marshmallow/ghost-like body** (like a small person in a white onesie), with **two short mitten arms** and **two short round feet**. She sits, stands and holds things with her arms like a person. **No four-legged pose.**

## Style
- Flat pastel colours, **thin clean dark-brown lines** (#3A2E33), almost no shading (at most one soft shadow tone), soft pink blush. Diary-comic / emoticon look — **not** glossy, not thick sticker outlines.
- For the HUD: transparent background, no frame, no text, square 256×256, subject filling ~85%.

## Composition rules
- **Small stat icons (20–24 px)**: the meaning prop is the hero (big, simple, bright, slightly thicker line than the character so it reads small); Doro peeks from behind/beside it (head + arms).
- **Equipment slot icons (56 px)**: Doro (head + upper body) holds the item. One-handed items: in her **right arm (viewer's left)** for the R slot and a separate **left-arm (viewer's right)** version for the L slot. Two-handed items: held with both arms.

## Common mistakes
One-sided bun, purple ribbons, glasses, four legs/crawling, thick glossy sticker outlines, gear covering the eyes or buns.
